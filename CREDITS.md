The environment layout, the stream, the pond, the bridge and the grass are generated procedurally in code; ground materials use the Poly Haven textures above. The houses, well, fence, columns, dais and tents are modelled procedurally in Blender by `tools/props/build_props.py`. Characters are generated by
`tools/blender/build_character.py` with Blender + MPFB2; the skeleton warrior's bones are modelled by `tools/blender/bone_body.py` and driven by the same rig.
